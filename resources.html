Volunteer Safety Guidelines

  • Wear closed-toe shoes when gardening.
  • Bring water and stay hydrated.
  • Apply sunscreen during outdoor events.
  • Use gardening tools responsibly.
  • Follow garden coordinator instructions.

Seasonal Gardening Calendar

  • Spring: Plant lettuce, spinach, peas, and herbs.
  • Summer: Grow tomatoes, peppers, cucumbers, and beans.
  • Fall: Plant kale, carrots, and broccoli.
  • Winter: Plan next season and prepare compost.

Quick Gardening Tips

Start Small

Begin with a few plants and gradually expand as you gain experience.

Water Consistently

Deep, regular watering is often better than frequent shallow watering.

Support Pollinators

Plant flowers that attract bees, butterflies, and other pollinators.

Compost Organics

Food scraps and yard waste can become nutrient-rich compost.
