Event Information

What should I bring?

Volunteers should wear comfortable clothing, closed-toe shoes, sunscreen, and bring a reusable water bottle.


Are events beginner-friendly?

Yes. Most events welcome first-time volunteers and provide tools and instruction.


How do I register?

Click the Register button next to any event to sign up and receive event details.
