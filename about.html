About Me

"There are no straight lines in nature." - Gaudi

My journey has been anything but linear. I studied landscape design and behavioral economics, developed financial models, designed cafe menus, and I'm currently researching Gen Z manifestation content.

I was in a phase of wanting to understand what genuinely motivates me outside of structured goals and external expectations. I didn’t just want new hobbies—I wanted to see how I responded to unfamiliar environments, physical learning curves, solitude, and social settings where I had no existing identity or role to fall back on.

I intentionally explored a mix of hands-on crafts, physical activities, and immersive travel experiences, including crochet, film photography, ceramics, rock climbing, ballroom dancing, and solo travel across Asia. Each experience pushed me into a different mode of learning—slow and repetitive skill-building, embodied physical trust, structured partner coordination, and navigating unfamiliar cultures independently. I approached each one as a way to observe how I learn, adapt, and engage with uncertainty in real time.

This exploration strengthened my ability to learn new skills quickly, stay present in discomfort, and adapt across very different environments. I built greater confidence in navigating ambiguity, improved my observation of my own behavioral patterns in new settings, and developed a stronger sense of how context shapes engagement and learning.
