Accessibility & FAQ

Community Harvest is committed to making volunteer opportunities, gardening resources, and community events accessible to everyone.

Volunteer Accessibility Information

  • Many gardens are accessible by public transportation.
  • Some gardens provide wheelchair-accessible pathways.
  • Volunteers may request accommodations when registering for events.
  • Garden coordinators can provide additional accessibility details.
  • Family-friendly and beginner-friendly opportunities are identified throughout the site.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need gardening experience?

No. Many volunteer opportunities are beginner-friendly and provide training on-site.

What should I bring to volunteer events?

Comfortable clothing, closed-toe shoes, sunscreen, and a reusable water bottle are recommended.

Are community garden events free?

Most volunteer opportunities and community events are free to attend unless otherwise noted.

Can children participate?

Many gardens welcome families and children. Check the event description for age recommendations.

How do I find accessible gardens?

Accessibility information is included in garden listings whenever available. You may also contact garden coordinators directly for specific accommodation requests.

How can a garden join Community Harvest?

Visit the Contact page and reach out to our team to learn about becoming a partner garden.
