About Community Harvest

Connecting volunteers with community gardens to build stronger, healthier, and more sustainable neighborhoods throughout Philadelphia.

Our Mission

Community Harvest serves as a bridge between individuals who want to make a difference and community gardens that need support. Our mission is to make volunteer opportunities more visible, accessible, and welcoming to people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Why We Created Community Harvest

Community gardens provide neighborhoods with fresh food, environmental education, green public spaces, and opportunities for social connection. However, many gardens struggle to recruit and coordinate volunteers because information is often scattered across social media, flyers, word-of-mouth, and individual organization websites.


Community Harvest was designed to solve this problem by creating a centralized hub where volunteers can easily discover local gardens, learn about upcoming events, access educational resources, and become active members of their communities.

Our Vision

We envision a future where every resident has easy access to volunteer opportunities, community green spaces, and educational resources that promote sustainability, environmental stewardship, and neighborhood engagement.
